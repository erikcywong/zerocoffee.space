Carbon method & MRV

What this platform will and will not claim. Read this before you invest or invest through us.

Investing is not buying carbon. A carbon asset only exists once a reduction or removal is additional, measured, third-party verified, registered, and transferred or retired on a registry. Equity produces none of this automatically.

The six-step dMRV workflow

01

Data capture

Manual entry for smallholder contexts, API and IoT ingestion for mills, roasteries and warehouses.

02

Standardisation

Activity data mapped to methodology-specific parameters with source and applicability flags.

03

Quality control

Missing-field detection, outlier flags and an append-only audit trail on every edit.

04

Carbon calculation

Baseline and project emissions computed against the selected methodology, with an explicit uncertainty band.

05

Independent verification

A VVB reviews the monitoring report through a dedicated portal and issues approve / conditional / reject.

06

Registry issuance

Tonnes are issued, transferred or retired on the registry. The platform records entitlements; it is not itself a registry.

Use tags — the double-counting firewall

Every verified tonne carries exactly one tag at any moment. Changing a tag is a workflow, not a dropdown.

TagMeaningCan it be sold or reassigned?
Scope 3 insetClaimed by an enterprise as a supply-chain reduction within its own boundaryNo — locked to that enterprise
Offset retirementRetired on a registry against residual emissionsNo — retirement is irreversible
HeldAllocated to a rights holder, not yet retired or claimedOnly via the entitlement contract
AvailableUnallocated platform reserve or project balanceYes, subject to contract terms

Retirement rules

  • Retirement is executed on the registry, not in a platform database. If the registry API is unavailable, the instruction enters a manual retirement pending state — the platform never shows a retirement as complete before the registry confirms it.
  • Every retirement certificate records project name, vintage, tonnage, standard, purpose and beneficiary (if the buyer elects to be named).
  • Once executed, the ledger row is locked. No edits, no reversal, no deletion.
Disclosure is support, not assurance. The platform prepares disclosure datasets. It does not certify that an enterprise's disclosures comply with any framework. Final responsibility sits with the enterprise and its auditors and legal advisers.
